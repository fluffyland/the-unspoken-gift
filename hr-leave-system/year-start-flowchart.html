Start a new year

What the button actually does, in order. HR Console → Company settings → Start a new year.

Before anything is written

▸
You press the button
Nothing is saved yet. LeaveDesk works out every number and shows you the preview.
Do the numbers look right?
No — close itNothing was written. Change what you need (someone's Carry Forward AL Maximum Day, a pending application) and press it again.
Yes — confirmNow it writes. Everything below happens, once, for each employee still working here.

Then, for each employee — in this order

0
Already done this year?
If this person was processed for this year before, skip them entirely. This is what makes pressing the button twice safe.
1
Write off expired carry-forward
Days carried in earlier that have passed their expiry date are removed now, so they cannot carry a second time.
2
Count what is left of last year's annual leave
Entitled minus taken. One pot — any surviving carry-forward is in there too.
3
Carry forward, up to their maximum
Whatever fits under their Carry Forward AL Maximum Day moves into the new year.
4
Forfeit the rest
Anything above the maximum is written off. LeaveDesk does not pay it out.
5
Stamp an expiry date on what carried
Set by Carry Forward AL expire after (months). Blank = the days never expire.
6
Clear every leave type that starts fresh
Sick, hospitalisation and so on go to zero, whatever was left in them.
7
Write it down, permanently
One row in Past runs with their name, what carried, what was forfeited, what expired. Kept forever, never overwritten.

Last, once everybody has been through

8
Credit the new year's allowance
Everybody gets their annual leave for the new year — pro-rated for anyone who joined part-way through.
DonePeople open LeaveDesk and see their new allowance, plus anything that carried, with its use-by date.
Why step 8 is last. If the new allowance were credited before step 6, step 6 would clear it away the moment it arrived. The order is not optional.

What if you press it twice?

Nothing happens twice. Step 0 catches it. Press Start 2026 again today and it reports 0 employees processed — nothing carried, nothing forfeited, nothing cleared, nothing credited.

Same if it stops halfway — a laptop closing, the internet dropping. Press it again and it finishes the people it never reached, leaving everyone else exactly as they are.

What it never touches

  • Off-in-lieu — never cleared, never expired, never credited here. It is earned day by day and stays until used.
  • Anyone who has left — offboarded employees are skipped entirely.
  • Leave already taken — days used last year stay used. Nothing is refunded.
  • Leave types set not to reset yearly — those keep their balance.
  • The past — last year's records, applications and approvals are never altered.
