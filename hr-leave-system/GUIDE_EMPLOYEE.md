# LeaveDesk — Employee Guide

How to book leave. Should take about two minutes to read.

---

## Signing in for the first time

Go to the LeaveDesk link HR sent you.

Sign in with **your work email** and the temporary password HR gave you —
usually `Ssu123@`.

**Change it straight away:** 🔑 **Password** button, top right.

## Forgotten your password?

You can reset it yourself — no need to wait for HR.

1. On the sign-in page, tap **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your work email, tap **Send code**
3. Check your email for a **6-digit code** (check spam if it's not there)
4. Type the code, tap **Verify**
5. Choose a new password, twice, and you're in

The code expires after about 10 minutes, so use it promptly. If it runs out, just
ask for another.

**Still stuck?** If you can't get into your work email either, ask HR — they can
reset it for you the old way.

> You'll get an email whenever your password changes. If one arrives and it wasn't
> you, tell HR straight away.

## What the tabs do

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | your balances and anything awaiting action |
| **My details** | your profile, team, approver, account type |
| **Apply** | book leave |
| **My applications** | everything you've submitted and where it's up to |
| **Calendar** | company calendar and public holidays |

If you approve leave for other people you'll also see **Approvals**.

## Applying for leave

**Apply** tab.

**1. Leave type.** Once picked, your available balance appears underneath —
broken down into this year's days, anything carried forward, used, and pending.

**2. Start and end date.** The system totals the working days for you.
**Weekends and Singapore public holidays are excluded automatically** — a
Friday-to-Monday over a long weekend won't cost you the holiday.

**3. Half days (optional).** For leave types that allow it, each day defaults to
a full day. Change any single day to **Morning only (AM)** or
**Afternoon only (PM)**.

**4. Remarks — required.** A brief reason. Your approver sees this.

**5. Attachment.**
- **Sick leave: your MC is required.** Attach a photo or PDF, and hand the
  **original hardcopy to HR within 3 working days**.
- Other types: optional, but helpful — a flight booking, an appointment letter.
- Only you, your approver and HR can open it.

**6. Submit application.**

### Things the form will tell you

- **Insufficient balance** — you've asked for more days than you have
- **Next year is view-only** — next year's leave opens on 1 January, when that
  year's balance is granted. You can already look at next year's public holidays
  in **Calendar**
- **Backdated** — your start date is in the past. Normal for sick leave

## After you submit

**My applications** shows the status of everything:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | waiting for your approver |
| **Approved** | done — days deducted |
| **Returned** | your approver wants a change. Open it, read the comment, edit, resubmit |
| **Rejected** | declined, with a reason |
| **Withdrawn** | you cancelled it before a decision |
| **Cancellation requested** | you've asked to cancel approved leave |
| **Cancelled** | approved leave successfully cancelled, days returned |

Some requests need **two** approvals. It stays Pending after the first — that's
normal, not a fault.

## Changing your mind

There are two different things, depending on whether it's been approved yet:

| Situation | Button | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Not approved yet | **Withdraw** | Gone immediately. No approval needed. |
| Already approved | **Cancel this leave** | Goes to your approver to confirm |

Both are in the popup — open the application from **My applications** first.

**If you have no approver** (the Managing Director, or anyone whose leave is approved
automatically), there is nobody to send the request to — so the cancellation is
**confirmed on the spot** and the days come straight back. The timeline records it as
*"Cancelled automatically — no approver needed"*.

### Cancelling leave you've already started

You can. Cancelling gives you back **the whole application's days** — including any
you already took.

So if you came back early, you must **apply again for the days you were actually
away**, or your balance will be higher than it should be. The system tells you this
on screen and offers an **Apply for days taken** button, which opens the form already
filled in with your original dates — change the end date to your last day away and
submit.

Everything asks before it acts. Cancelling shows you the leave type, the dates and the
number of days first, with **Go back** and **Confirm Cancellation**; nothing is
changed until you confirm. Afterwards a message tells you exactly what happened and
how many days went back into your balance.

*Example:* you booked 23 Jul – 20 Aug and came back on the 18th. Cancel it (all 16
days return), then re-apply for 23 Jul – 17 Aug. Your balance ends up correct.

> If you skip the re-application, you'll be holding days you actually used. Your
> approver and HR can both see it, so it will come out — do the second step.

**Returned to you** — open it, read the comment, fix it, resubmit. It keeps its
history; you're not starting again.

## Checking your balance

**Overview**, or the **Apply** tab once you've picked a leave type.

Your balance shows:

- **Allowance** — everything credited to you: this year's grant, anything carried
  forward, plus any corrections HR has made
- **Used** — everything deducted: leave taken, and any corrections
- **Pending** — requested but not yet decided; already set aside for you

**Available** is what you can actually book today — Allowance minus Used minus
Pending. The same three figures appear in **My details → My leave entitlement**, and
they will always agree.

Carried-forward days **expire**, so spend them first. When you have any, Overview
shows the amount and the date they run out; they are already included in the figures
above.

### Why your annual leave might go up every month

Some companies credit the whole year's annual leave on 1 January. Others credit it
**monthly, as you earn it** — so in March you would have roughly three months' worth,
not the full year. Both are normal; ask HR which one applies here.

**On the monthly method your Available can show a minus figure**, for example
**−1.5 days**. That is not a fault. It means you have taken more than you have earned
so far, and the next months' credits will bring it back up. You are not being charged
for it — but you may not be able to book more leave until it recovers, and if you
leave the company with a negative balance it is normally settled in your final pay.

## Common questions

**Do I lose a public holiday inside my leave?**
No. Weekends and Singapore public holidays are excluded automatically.

**Can I book half a day?**
For leave types that allow it, yes — set any day to Morning or Afternoon.

**Can I book next year's leave now?**
Not until 1 January, when next year's balance is granted. Next year's public
holidays are already visible in **Calendar**.

**I forgot my password.**
Reset it yourself from the sign-in page — see above. Ask HR only if you can't get
into your work email either.

**Do carried-forward days expire?**
Yes. Use them before this year's allowance — the system already does that for
you when it counts.

**My application has sat Pending for days.**
Chase your approver directly. LeaveDesk doesn't escalate on its own.

**The site loads but I can't sign in.**
Tell HR — it's usually the database, not your account.
