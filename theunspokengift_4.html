About Us

Every gift has an
unspoken feeling.

The Unspoken Gift was born from a simple belief — that the most meaningful things in life are often left unsaid. A gift isn't just an object. It's the warmth behind it, the thought that went into choosing it, and the love it carries from one person to another.

We started as a small home-based business in Singapore, handcrafting gift boxes for friends and family. What began as a labour of love quickly grew as more people wanted to give something truly personal — something that said more than words ever could.

Today, every box we curate is still made with the same care and intention as the very first one. We source thoughtfully, wrap beautifully, and personalise every detail — because we believe your loved ones deserve nothing less.

Our Values

Intentional gifting. We don't do generic. Every box is curated with the recipient in mind.

Local with love. Singapore-based, family-run, and proud of it.

Sustainable where possible. We choose quality over quantity, always.

Every box we make can be fully customised to suit your occasion, your recipient, and your budget. Here's how the process works:

1
Tell us about the recipientShare with us who the gift is for — their personality, preferences, dietary needs, and what the occasion means to you.
2
Set your budgetWe work with all budgets starting from $45. Let us know your range and we'll curate the best possible box within it.
